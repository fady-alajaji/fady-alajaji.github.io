Friday, August 1, 1997

Room 210, Walter Light Hall
Queen's University



The Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Queen's University is organizing ``The L. Lorne Campbell Conference on Communications'' on Friday, August 1, 1997.
The conference is in honor of Professor Emeritus L. Lorne Campbell, and in recognition for his oustanding academic career and his significant contributions to the university and the international Mathematics and Engineering communities.
